Pilot programme

Start small. Measure. Then scale.

We begin with a short pilot on selected sites. When it ends you hold baseline scores, photo-verified findings and a roll-out plan built on real field data.

Why a pilot

Proof in the field before the contract.

Limits risk

You start with selected sites instead of the whole network. Scope, frequency and budget are settled on real data.

Produces real data

A baseline score, photo-verified finding list and prioritised actions for every site. Measurement, not assumptions.

Plans the roll-out

In the pilot review meeting, regions, visit frequency, teams and budget are decided together.

Pilot process

A pilot in six steps.

01

Scope and criteria

Site set, category scope, question set and score weights are approved.

02

Baseline measurement

Current condition is recorded with photos and checklists.

03

Field execution

CLEAN, CARE, AUDIT or EV scope delivered by regional teams.

04

Results report

Site scores, critical findings and action proposals are reported.

05

Second round

Actions are closed; the second visit verifies score and open-finding progress.

06

Roll-out plan

A scaling plan with brand standards, SLA targets, regions and calendar.

Pilot outputs

What do you have when the pilot ends?

  • Audit summary and category scores
  • Photo-verified finding list: critical, high, medium, low
  • Site and regional comparison
  • Closure evidence with before and after photos
  • Estimated workload, visit frequency and implementation calendar
  • Verified unit times and a settled scope for the quote
