What personal data we process

The site collects only the information you enter in the forms yourself. You may leave optional fields blank.

  • Identity and contact details: full name, email address, phone number
  • Company details: the company you work for
  • Request details: number of sites, solution or starting product of interest, sector, city/region distribution, target start date, and anything you write in the message or notes field
  • Transaction details: form type (quote or pilot), the language of the form and the time of submission
  • Technical data: connection data such as IP address, browser information and access time that is automatically recorded in our service providers' server logs when you access the site and the form server

Why we process your personal data

  • To receive and assess your quote and pilot requests and respond to you
  • To schedule a scoping meeting and prepare the pilot scope and proposal
  • To contact you by phone or email about your request
  • To conduct pre-contract discussions and manage request records
  • To prevent misuse of the forms and automated (spam) submissions, and keep the site and our information secure
  • To meet our legal obligations and respond to authorised public bodies when required

Legal grounds and method of collection

Your personal data is collected electronically, directly from you, through the forms on bigel360.com. Technical connection data is generated automatically when you access the site.

We process this data on the following legal grounds under Article 5(2) of the KVKK:

  • Processing is necessary for entering into or performing a contract, where the data belongs to the parties to that contract (Art. 5(2)(c))
  • Processing is necessary for BİGEL to comply with a legal obligation (Art. 5(2)(ç))
  • Processing is necessary for BİGEL's legitimate interests, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (Art. 5(2)(f))

We keep your personal data for as long as the purpose of processing requires and for the periods set by applicable law. When these periods end, the data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

Quote and pilot requests that do not lead to a contract are kept for no longer than [RETENTION PERIOD] after the request is closed. Where a request leads to a contract, the data is kept for the duration of the contractual relationship and any statutory retention period.

Data security

BİGEL takes appropriate administrative and technical measures to prevent unlawful processing of and access to personal data and to keep it secure. Only authorised users can access form submissions in the panel, and data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Your rights under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you may apply to BİGEL to:

  • Find out whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information about the processing if it is
  • Find out the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose
  • Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data has been transferred
  • Ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected
  • Ask for your data to be erased or destroyed under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK
  • Ask for third parties who received your data to be notified of any correction, erasure or destruction
  • Object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated analysis of your data
  • Claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing
