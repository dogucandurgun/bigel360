Audit categories

A common audit framework adapted to each industry.

Each item is marked Compliant, Non-compliant or Not applicable, and evidenced with photo, location, date and notes.

01

Brand Audit

Logo, corporate colours, current signage, campaign material and price communication. BRAND SCORE.

02

Visual Audit

Signage, totems, illuminated letters, vinyl, posters, shopfronts, stands and digital screens.

03

Clean Audit

Floors, glass, façade, car park, waste areas and common areas; colour-coded summary.

04

Physical Audit

Walls, doors, furniture, barriers, railings and minor damage; converts to maintenance work orders.

05

Customer Experience Audit

Visibility, entrance, wayfinding, checkout and waiting areas. CX SCORE.

Digital audit product

AUDIT 100 — 100 criteria. One score.

10 categories, 100 checkpoints. Each item scores Compliant 100, Partial 50 or Non-compliant 0; category weights produce a site score out of 100. The photo rule is defined per question: conditional, mandatory or before-and-after.
