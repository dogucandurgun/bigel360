Scope

What does fuel station cleaning cover?

BİGEL CLEAN covers the customer-facing exterior of the station with a defined method, equipment and photo angle for each area. Modules can be one-off or part of a periodic programme.

Fuel station canopy cleaning

Periodic removal of atmospheric dirt, dust, exhaust marks, soot and biological soiling from the canopy ceiling and fascia.

Totem and price sign cleaning

Surface-appropriate cleaning of totems, signage and wayfinding, so the brand stays visible from the road.

Pump island and forecourt cleaning

Dirt, oil stains and litter on concrete and asphalt around pump islands and across the forecourt, all tied to one checklist.

Façade, glass and car park

Entrance glass, curtain walls, composite and metal surfaces on the station building and shop front; dirt and oil stains on car park surfaces.

Night, emergency and seasonal cleaning

Night Clean is scheduled after closing without disrupting operations. Emergency Clean handles spills, bad weather, vandalism and pre-audit needs; Seasonal Clean targets winter grime, leaves, mud and slip risks.

Site safety

Orderly, safe work on a site that never stops.

At a fuel station, cleaning and maintenance happen while customers keep coming. Visit plans are built around the station's operating pattern, and safety items are scored separately in the audit.

  • Visit hours, access conditions and target times are agreed during scope and SLA definition
  • Method, equipment and chemical use follow the standard operating procedure
  • Teams are trained to a common service standard and assessed on uniform, ID and safety standards
  • Rapid response to spills and sudden soiling with Emergency Clean
  • Seasonal Clean programme against slip risk in winter
  • In AUDIT 100, Safety & Risk carries a 15% weight; critical findings trigger an instant alert
Multi-site networks

More stations, the same cleaning standard.

BİGEL's journey began with exterior cleaning and minor maintenance for fuel station networks. The lesson was clear: doing the work is not enough; every site has to hold the same standard, and head office needs proof.

  • One service definition, checklist and execution standard for every station
  • Central task and visit planning; monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly or seasonal programmes
  • Standard photo angles and quality criteria
  • Performance visibility by site, region and period
  • On-time visits, completion rate and action closure time tracked through KPIs and SLAs

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does fuel station cleaning cover?

Canopy ceiling and fascia, totems and signage, pump islands and forecourt, building façade and glass, car park and station surroundings. Scope is defined per station with a checklist.

Can canopy and totem cleaning be done while the station is open?

Visit hours and access conditions are set around the station's operating pattern during scoping. Where needed, Night Clean is scheduled after closing without disrupting operations.

How often should forecourts and pump islands be cleaned?

Frequency is set as a monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly or seasonal programme based on traffic and surface condition. Emergency Clean covers sudden spills and soiling; the final frequency is confirmed with pilot data.

Do we need to call separate tradespeople for minor repairs?

No. Station Care brings minor work on canopies, signage, pump islands, kerbs and barriers into one work-order flow. Work requiring legal certification, such as electrical or mechanical installations, is coordinated with qualified specialists while BİGEL keeps tracking it.

How is completed cleaning and maintenance verified?

Every visit is recorded with before and after photos, GPS and a timestamp and verified against the checklist and mandatory photos. All records are tracked per station in FIELD360.

How do you keep one cleaning standard across many stations?

Every station works to the same service definition, checklist and photo angles; visits are planned centrally and delivered by regional teams reaching all 81 provinces. Results are compared by site and region.

How are audit findings closed?

An AUDIT 100 finding becomes a CARE work order with a priority, owner and due date. It closes with an after photo, and the second visit verifies score progress.
