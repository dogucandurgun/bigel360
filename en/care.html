BİGEL CARE — Site maintenance and field response

Protect the site before it breaks.

We manage the minor maintenance, repair and field response needs of brand sites from one centre. Detection, planning, on-site execution, photography and closure tracking are all handled by BİGEL.

Scope

Small jobs get fixed before they pile up.

Small physical problems accumulating at sites hurt customer experience and brand appearance. BİGEL CARE manages them through a single work-order system.

  • Periodic and preventive maintenance
  • Fault and physical damage response
  • Visual area and brand element care
  • Exterior and surroundings care
  • Furniture, equipment and minor assembly
  • Emergency field response

Work requiring specialist skills or legal certification (electrical, mechanical installations, etc.) is coordinated with qualified specialists; BİGEL manages detection, planning and closure tracking.
