How we work

We make sure the work planned at the centre happens correctly in the field.

We start with a needs analysis, validate with a pilot on selected sites, and scale to regional and nationwide operations.

Regular operations in six steps.

01

Needs and site analysis

Site network, service needs and priorities.

02

Service scope and SLA

Checklists, evidence rules and target times.

03

Pilot

A real field test at selected sites.

04

Results and action plan

Score, workload, team and visit frequency.

05

Roll-out

Regional go-live and a regular calendar.

06

Regular reporting

Task, report, action and performance reviews.

Implementation timeline

From pilot to roll-out in eight weeks.

Weeks 1–2

Preparation

  • Site list and service scope
  • Checklists and photo standard
  • Team plan and communication flow
  • SLA targets
Weeks 3–4

Pilot

  • Site visits and photo records
  • Site scores
  • Finding classification and action proposals
  • Pilot review meeting
Weeks 5–8

Roll-out

  • Team capacity plan
  • Regional go-live
  • Regular service calendar
  • Dashboard, KPI and SLA tracking
Quality assurance model

Every execution is verified.

  • Pre-task team and scope check
  • Execution to the standard operating procedure
  • Mandatory photo and checklist verification
  • Central quality control and non-conformity record
  • Corrective action, owner and deadline tracking
  • Regular KPI and SLA review
Digital reporting

What do management teams see?

  • Visit date, site and team information
  • Question-level scores and photo evidence
  • Red, amber and green status summary
  • Priority findings and assigned actions
  • Site, region and period comparisons
  • Management summary and closure tracking
Scalable roll-out

From pilot to nationwide.

1 · Pilot

Validating the standard at selected sites: scope, baseline measurement, field execution, results report.

2 · Regional roll-out

Taking the model to priority regions: team planning, training, SLA tracking, regional performance.

3 · Nationwide

Managing the whole network with one model: central coordination, site scores, work orders, management dashboard.
