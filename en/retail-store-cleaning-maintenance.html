Shopfronts, façades and signage to the same standard in every store.

Scope

What does retail chain cleaning cover?

BİGEL CLEAN focuses on what customers see from outside: shopfront, entrance, façade, signage and surroundings. Each area has a defined method, equipment and photo angle; modules run one-off or as a periodic programme.

Shopfront and entrance glass

Glass & Façade Clean covers shopfront and entrance glass on a planned visit calendar, recorded with the same checklist and photo angles in every store.

Façade and composite surfaces

Curtain walls, composite, metal, stone and painted surfaces are cleaned with methods suited to each surface type.

Signage, illuminated letters and wayfinding

Signage & Totem Clean covers signs, totems and wayfinding that affect brand visibility, with surface-appropriate cleaning.

Entrance and store surroundings

Outdoor flooring at the entrance and around the store is handled for dirt and stains under Floor & Car Park Clean.

After closing and emergency response

Night Clean is scheduled after closing hours without disrupting operations. Emergency Clean responds to sudden soiling, bad weather, vandalism or pre-inspection needs.

Scope is adapted to each store's surfaces and operating hours. This page covers exterior and shopfront services.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does retail chain store cleaning cover?

Shopfront and entrance glass, façade surfaces (curtain wall, composite, metal, stone, painted), signs, totems and wayfinding, and outdoor flooring at the entrance and around the store. Night Clean and Emergency Clean can be added.

Can shopfront and façade cleaning be done while the store is open?

Visit times are planned around each store's operating hours. For busy stores, Night Clean moves the work to after closing so it does not disrupt operations.

How are all stores checked after a campaign or signage change?

A project audit checks stores in bulk after a campaign, opening or signage change. Brand and Visual Audit items cover campaign material, price communication, signage, shopfronts and digital screens, and every item is evidenced with photos.

How do you keep the same visual standard across franchise stores?

BİGEL works with head office under one contract and one service definition. Every store uses a shared checklist, standard photo angles and quality criteria; store scores and non-conformities are tracked on a comparative dashboard for head office.

How do you verify that the cleaning was done?

Each visit includes before and after photos from standard angles, with GPS and a time stamp. The checklist, after photo and any reported findings form the completion record.

Can minor damage in the store be fixed too?

Yes. BİGEL CARE's Retail & Mall Care package covers shelving, display units and shopfront frames, from detection to photo-verified closure. Work requiring authorisation, such as electrical, is coordinated with qualified specialists.

Where in Türkiye do you operate?

BİGEL works across Türkiye with a regional field team network. Central planning and quality control are run from Istanbul; execution is carried out by regional teams.
