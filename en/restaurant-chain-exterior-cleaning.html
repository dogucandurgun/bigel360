Scope

What does restaurant exterior cleaning cover?

BİGEL CLEAN handles the guest-facing exterior of each branch with a defined method, equipment and photo angle. Modules can be applied once or added to a periodic programme.

Façade and glass

Shopfronts, entrance glass and curtain walls; composite, metal, stone and painted surfaces. The Glass & Façade Clean module uses a method suited to each surface.

Signage, totems and wayfinding

Restaurant sign cleaning directly affects brand visibility. Signs, totems and wayfinding are cleaned with surface-appropriate methods, so the branch stays legible from the road.

Drive-thru lane and car park

Dirt, oil stains and litter on concrete, asphalt, pavers, epoxy and stone across the drive-thru lane, car park and entrance. Delivered through the Floor & Car Park Clean module.

Outdoor seating furniture

Regular cleaning of the outdoor seating area and its furniture. Loose, damaged or missing parts are routed to a CARE work order under Furniture Care.

Seasonal programme: leaves, mud, winter grime

Seasonal Clean builds a programme around winter grime, moss, leaves, mud and slip risks. Emergency Clean covers sudden soiling, spills, bad weather or vandalism.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does restaurant façade and sign cleaning cover?

The façade and glass, shopfront and entrance glass, signs, totems and wayfinding, the outdoor seating area, the drive-thru lane, the car park and the entrance surroundings. Scope is defined for each branch with a checklist; modules run once or as a periodic programme.

Does drive-thru lane and car park cleaning disrupt service hours?

It is planned to keep disruption to a minimum. Visit times, access conditions and target durations are agreed at the scope and SLA stage. Night Clean is cleaning scheduled after closing without disrupting operations; for branches open late, timing is set together with the branch.

How does head office track the exterior of franchise branches?

Every branch is visited with the same checklist and standard photo angles. Each visit is recorded with before and after photos, GPS and a timestamp. Site scores, non-conformities and open work orders are tracked on a comparative dashboard for head office, and franchise audit measures standard compliance on its behalf.

Does the cleaning programme change with the seasons?

Yes. Seasonal Clean builds a programme around winter grime, moss, leaves, mud and slip risks. Sudden soiling, spills, bad weather or vandalism are handled outside the plan with Emergency Clean.

Is kitchen or hood cleaning included?

No. BİGEL's restaurant scope covers the façade, glass, signage, outdoor seating and drive-thru, plus minor interior maintenance jobs. Kitchen, hood and food-hygiene services are out of scope.

How does a pilot start for a restaurant chain?

A pilot typically runs on 20–30 sites over a 4–6 week period. Scope and criteria are approved, a baseline measurement is taken, and a second round verifies action closure and score progress. You end with baseline scores, photo-backed findings and a roll-out plan.
